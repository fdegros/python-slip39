A Paper Wallet holds the extended keys of one cryptocurrency account:
the extended public key (xpub, ypub or zpub), marked WATCH-ONLY, in the
visible part, and the extended private key (xprv, yprv or zprv), marked
SPEND, in the part that folds over.

If you import these SLIP-39 (or BIP-39) Mnemonics directly into your
Trezor hardware wallet – you do /not/ need Paper Wallets: you have
access to /all/ of the wallets, in every cryptocurrency supported by the
Trezor.

These are intended to support the importing of an /individual/ account
into software wallets that accept an extended key (eg. Electrum,
Sparrow, and other software wallets on PCs or mobile devices).  If you
need some cryptocurrency "walking around" money, use them to create
recoverable "Safe Portable Crypto Accounts" (below).

NOTE: you likely want to change the Custom Derivation Path in "# to
Derive:" to something other than your main account's addresses at
…/0'/0/0: probably a path like `99'/0/0-3', to create the Paper Wallet
for a different "account" (99'), which derives the addresses 0 to 3 of
that account.


1 Extended Private Key
══════════════════════

  Paper Wallets are *not* password-protected: there is no standard way
  to encrypt an extended private key.  Anyone who can read the extended
  private key can spend /every/ address of the account.  Fold the Paper
  Wallet over, to hide the extended private key, and keep it safe.

  If a Paper Wallet is lost, the Seed can still be recovered by
  collecting sufficient numbers of Mnemonics (cards), and then new Paper
  Wallets can be issued.


2 Safe Portable Crypto Accounts
═══════════════════════════════

  If you want to issue temporary Paper Wallets (to carry cryptocurrency
  with you to give to someone or buy something), then Paper Wallets
  derived from your account are a good idea.


2.1 Custom Derivation Path
──────────────────────────

  Use the `SLIP-39' App to recover your Seed, select the desired
  Cryptocurrency (eg. `BTC'), and then enter a derivation path "suffix"
  into the "# to Derive" field, eg. "/1'/0/0-2".

  For example, if you use the BIP-39 recovery "test" Mnemonic "zoo zoo
  zoo zoo zoo zoo zoo zoo zoo zoo zoo wrong", with the above "# to
  Derive", this would result in 1 Paper Wallet being produced, for the
  standard BTC Bech32 account at `m/84'/0'/1'', with the extended public
  key:

  zpub6rD5AGSXPTDMUaSe3aGDqWk4uMTwcrFwytkKuDGmi3ofUkJ4dQxXHZwiXWbHHrELJAor8xGs61F8sbKS2JdQkLZRnu5PGktmr6F32nEBUBb

  That account's 3 addresses are derived at the following standard
  paths:

  ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
   m/84'/0'/1'/0/0  bc1q8pqnqs573vx3qdp0xp6qdqzvnvy8px24rxh9lp 
   m/84'/0'/1'/0/1  bc1qwtc58u4mmnxa29u8j07e6lmqpnrs38vefy3y24 
   m/84'/0'/1'/0/2  bc1qg9s8qzm0lcetfv6umhlm3evtca5zsqv7elqd5s 
  ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  You could load these addresses with funds, write the amount on the
  front, and fold the Paper Wallet over twice (to hide the EXTENDED
  PRIVATE KEY, while leaving the EXTENDED PUBLIC KEY exposed), and even
  laminate it to avoid accidental discovery or exposure to moisture.
  Anyone who sees the extended public key can see all of the account's
  addresses and balances, but cannot spend them.

  Then, once loaded, they can always be *recovered* by you (if they are
  lost or accidentally destroyed).  Otherwise, the intended recipient
  can unfold the Paper Wallet, and move the funds to their own wallet.
